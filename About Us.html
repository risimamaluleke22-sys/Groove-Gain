About Groove & Gain

Who We Are

Groove & Gain is a music business dedicated to bringing music lovers, musicians and collectors closer to the music they love.

We specialise in selling music instrumentals, vinyl records and music-related products. Our goal is to provide quality products and services to people who have a passion for music.

Our History

Groove & Gain was created from a passion for music and the desire to provide musicians and music lovers with a place where they can discover quality music products.

The organisation started as a small music-focused business and aims to grow into a trusted destination for music enthusiasts.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide affordable and quality music products while creating an enjoyable experience for musicians, collectors and music lovers.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted and recognised music brand that connects people through music and provides quality music products and services.

Our Target Audience

  • Musicians
  • Music producers
  • Music collectors
  • Vinyl record enthusiasts
  • Music lovers
  • People looking for music instrumentals
